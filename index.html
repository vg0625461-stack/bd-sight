A Letter From
My Heart

Tap the envelope to open

Rose,

Un peru maariye neeyum romba azhagaana oru flower dhaan. Un cute-ah irukkura spring hair, un carefree-aana way-la life-a enjoy panradhu, adhe time-la chinna chinna vishayathukkum overthink panradhu ellame unnoda own beauty.

Sila neram nee romba carefree-ah iruppa, aana sila neram un mind-la aayiram thoughts odum. Aana enakku theriyum, adhuvum nee ellathayum romba deeply care panra person-ngradha kaattudhu. Especially, nee unakkaga mattum illa, unna suthi irukkura ellarukkagavum overcare pannuva. Unakku pidichavanga happy-ah irukanum nu nee nenachitu, avangaloda problems-a kooda un problem maadhiri eduthuppa. Adhaan un heart oda azhagu.

Un smile pathi sollanum na, adhu just oru smile illa… adhu oru light maadhiri. Nee sirikkumbodhu, unna suthi irukkura ellarukkum happiness spread aagum. Nee unmaiyileye everyone-oda sunshine, cheloowww.

Sila naal namma life romba dark-ah, confusing-ah irukkum. Appo Harry Potter-la sonna maadhiri, "Happiness can be found even in the darkest of times, if one only remembers to turn on the light." And enakku, andha light nee dhaan. En life-la sila dark moments irundhaalum, nee irukkumbodhu ellame konjam brighter-ah theriyudhu. Nee theriyama kooda enakku happiness kudukkura oru person.

So cheloww, un smile-a eppovume lose pannidadha, overthink pannumbodhu konjam rest eduthuko, ellarukkum nee kudukkura care-la konjam unakkum kuduthuko. Nee ellarukkum sunshine-ah irukkura maadhiri, enakku nee en light-ah irukka. Life evlo dark-ah irundhaalum, enakku light enga irukku nu theriyum… because ur my light.

Un peru Rose-na, enakku nee just oru beautiful flower illa; en life-la azhaga bloom aana, brightness-um happiness-um kondu vandha oru special person. 💌

— Gokul
